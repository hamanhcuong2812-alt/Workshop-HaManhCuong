**Topic** Querying job posting data with Athena.

**Week within the internship** 17/10/2026–23/10/2026.

## Week 11 objectives

- Understand how Athena uses the catalog to query S3 data.
- Read basic SQL queries and data filtering conditions.

## Week 11 learning outcomes

- Distinguish source data, metadata, and Athena query results.
- Understand that displayed statistics depend on dataset scope and filtering conditions.
